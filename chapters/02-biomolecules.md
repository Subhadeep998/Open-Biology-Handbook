# 02 · Biomolecules

[← Cell Biology](01-cell-biology.md) · [Contents](../README.md) · [Next: Enzymes & Metabolism →](03-enzymes-metabolism.md)

> **In one line:** four families of molecules — carbohydrates, lipids, proteins and nucleic acids — build every living thing.

## 1. The big four

| Class | Monomer | Bond | Main roles | Examples |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Carbohydrates | Monosaccharide | Glycosidic | Energy, storage, structure | Glucose, starch, glycogen, cellulose |
| Lipids | Fatty acids + glycerol (not true polymers) | Ester | Energy store, membranes, signalling | Triglycerides, phospholipids, steroids |
| Proteins | Amino acid | Peptide | Enzymes, structure, transport, signalling | Haemoglobin, collagen, insulin |
| Nucleic acids | Nucleotide | Phosphodiester | Information storage and transfer | DNA, RNA |

## 2. Carbohydrates

- **Monosaccharides:** glucose, fructose, galactose (hexoses); ribose, deoxyribose (pentoses).
- **Disaccharides:** sucrose = glucose + fructose · lactose = glucose + galactose · maltose = glucose + glucose.
- **Polysaccharides:**

| Polysaccharide | Linkage | Found in | Role |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Starch (amylose + amylopectin) | α-1,4 (+ α-1,6 branches) | Plants | Energy store |
| Glycogen | α-1,4 + more α-1,6 branches | Liver, muscle | Energy store |
| Cellulose | β-1,4 | Plant cell walls | Structure |
| Chitin | β-1,4 (N-acetylglucosamine) | Fungal walls, insect exoskeleton | Structure |

Humans lack cellulase, so β-1,4 cellulose passes through as dietary fibre.

## 3. Lipids

- **Saturated** fatty acids: no C=C, straight, solid at room temperature.
- **Unsaturated:** one or more C=C; *cis* bonds kink the chain → liquid oils.
- **Phospholipids:** hydrophilic phosphate head + two hydrophobic tails → bilayers.
- **Steroids:** four fused rings — cholesterol, testosterone, oestrogen, cortisol, vitamin D.

## 4. Proteins

**20 standard amino acids**, each with an amino group, carboxyl group, H and a variable **R group**.

| Level | What holds it | Description |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Primary | Peptide bonds | Amino-acid sequence |
| Secondary | H-bonds in the backbone | α-helix, β-pleated sheet |
| Tertiary | R-group interactions: H-bonds, ionic, hydrophobic, **disulphide** | Overall 3D fold |
| Quaternary | Same as tertiary, between chains | Multi-subunit proteins (haemoglobin = 2α + 2β) |

- **Denaturation** — heat, pH or chemicals unfold the protein; primary structure stays intact.
- **Anfinsen's experiment** (ribonuclease A) — sequence determines structure.
- **Essential amino acids** (adults, 9): His, Ile, Leu, Lys, Met, Phe, Thr, Trp, Val.

## 5. Nucleic acids

| | DNA | RNA |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Sugar | Deoxyribose | Ribose (2′-OH) |
| Bases | A, T, G, C | A, **U**, G, C |
| Strands | Usually double | Usually single |
| Stability | High | Lower (2′-OH makes it reactive) |
| Types | Genomic, mitochondrial | mRNA, tRNA, rRNA, miRNA, lncRNA… |

- **Chargaff's rules:** in dsDNA, A = T and G = C, so purines = pyrimidines.
- **Watson–Crick B-DNA:** right-handed, ~10.5 bp per turn, antiparallel strands; A–T has 2 H-bonds, G–C has 3.
- **Purines** (two rings): A, G. **Pyrimidines** (one ring): C, T, U. Memory aid: *"Pure As Gold"*, *"CUT the Py"*.

## 6. Water and pH — the background to everything

- Water is polar, forms H-bonds, has high specific heat and is the "universal solvent".
- pH = −log[H⁺]. Blood pH ≈ 7.35–7.45, buffered mainly by bicarbonate.
- **Henderson–Hasselbalch:** pH = pKa + log([A⁻]/[HA]).

## ⚠️ Common confusions

- Lipids are **not** polymers in the strict sense.
- "Glycogen is animal starch" — yes, but it is *more* branched.
- Denaturation does not break peptide bonds.

## 📝 Practice

1. Which linkage is found in cellulose? (a) α-1,4 (b) β-1,4 (c) α-1,6 (d) β-1,6
2. Disulphide bonds stabilise mainly which level of protein structure? (a) Primary (b) Secondary (c) Tertiary (d) None
3. If a dsDNA sample has 30% A, the % of G is: (a) 30 (b) 40 (c) 20 (d) 70
4. Which base is found in RNA but not DNA? (a) Adenine (b) Thymine (c) Uracil (d) Cytosine
5. Lactose is made of: (a) glucose + fructose (b) glucose + galactose (c) glucose + glucose (d) galactose + fructose

<details><summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

1 (b) · 2 (c) · 3 (c) — A = T = 30% → G + C = 40% → G = 20% · 4 (c) · 5 (b)
</details>
